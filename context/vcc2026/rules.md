# Rules affecting this repository

This is a factual record of the rules effective 2026-08-04, not legal interpretation.

- Participants must be at least 18. Teams contain 1–8 people; one team per person. Organization/university participation restrictions apply, including personal-capacity conditions.
- Public or private/proprietary data and pretrained models may be used only where the participant has the necessary rights. External compute is permitted subject to the same responsibility.
- Experimental or published results may train or fine-tune a model, but a submitted prediction may not incorporate lab results directly or be manually revised from published/laboratory results.
- Entrants must have rights to submitted data/code and are responsible for third-party licenses and IP.
- Validation leaderboard entries are limited to two scoring submissions/day until final release. Final D/E/F permits the same daily rate; only the last final submission is used.
- Finalists provide a high-level method description covering datasets, processing, models, non-learned components, training/inference, and optionally compute. Code and weights are not currently required by that disclosure clause.
- Team, discussion/publication, prize, eligibility, and organizer rights are governed by the full live rules. Rules may change with notice; always re-check before consequential actions.
